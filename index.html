About me

I'm an AI automation engineer. I design and build workflows that connect the tools a business already uses (Gmail, Google Sheets, Drive, Calendar, Telegram, forms) and add AI where it saves real time: reading documents, answering customers, qualifying leads and drafting content.

I come from an engineering background, so I treat each automation as a system: map the process first, build it to handle errors, test it with real scenarios, and hand it over with clear documentation your team can follow.
